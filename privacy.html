Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data CyCraftware holds when you use the CyInvoice invoicing app (invoices.cycraftware.com) or this website, why, where it is kept, and your rights.

1. Who we are

CyCraftware is run by Christos Diamantides, a sole trader based in Limassol, Cyprus.
Contact for anything about your data: info@cycraftware.com

2. Two kinds of data, two roles

Your account and business details. We decide how this data is used, so we are its controller. It includes:

  • your email address and password (the password is stored only in scrambled, unreadable form);
  • the business details you enter: name, address, phone, VAT and tax numbers, bank details and logo;
  • technical records needed to keep you logged in and the service secure.

Your customers' data. The customer names, addresses, VAT numbers, contact details, invoices, credit notes and payments you enter belong to you. For this data you are the controller and we act only as your processor (see section 9). We never use it for our own purposes, never sell it and never contact your customers.

3. Why we use your data

  • To provide the service you signed up for: logging in, creating and storing invoices, making PDFs, keeping backups.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping invoices for as long as invoicing and VAT law requires.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to the service. We do not send marketing emails without your agreement.

4. Where your data is kept

All CyInvoice data is stored in the European Union. We use these providers:

  • Supabase: database, login and logo storage, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • DigitalOcean: the server that runs the app, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Cloudflare (R2) and Backblaze (B2): copies of issued invoice and credit note PDFs, and nightly backups of the database, in EU data centres. Using two separate providers means your records survive if one of them fails.
  • European Commission VIES: when you enter a VAT number, it is checked against the EU's official VAT number register.

Some of these providers are companies based outside the EU. Your data stays stored in the EU, and they are bound by data protection agreements that include the EU's standard contractual clauses.

This website itself has no forms, analytics or tracking.

5. How long we keep it

  • Invoices, credit notes and payments cannot be deleted in CyInvoice, only cancelled or voided, because the law requires business records to be kept. They are kept for at least 6 years.
  • If you close your account, we delete your account and business details, except records the law requires to be kept. Deleted data disappears from our backups as older backups are replaced.
  • Before closing an account you can download a full copy of your data (see section 7).

6. Cookies

We use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, on this website or in the app.

  • Security cookie (always): the app uses a small cookie that protects file uploads, such as your logo, from misuse. The app needs it to work.
  • "Keep me logged in" cookie (only if you choose it): if you tick "Keep me logged in on this device" at login, one cookie stored in your browser for up to 30 days keeps you logged in when you refresh or reopen the page. It is off unless you tick the box, and it is deleted when you log out.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data. You can also do this yourself at any time in Business Settings → Export your data, which downloads everything as spreadsheet files;
  • correct data that is wrong, which you can also do in the app;
  • delete your data, apart from records the law requires us to keep;
  • restrict or object to how we use it.

Email info@cycraftware.com. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Cyprus data protection authority, the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection: www.dataprotection.gov.cy

8. Security

  • All connections are encrypted (https).
  • Each business can see only its own data; this is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app.
  • Backups are made every night to two separate providers.
  • If a data breach happens that puts your data at risk, we will tell you without undue delay and report it to the Commissioner within 72 hours, as the law requires.

9. When we process data for you (processor terms)

For the customer data you enter into CyInvoice, we:

  • process it only to provide CyInvoice to you, following your use of the app;
  • keep it confidential;
  • use only the providers listed in section 4, and tell you before adding a new one;
  • help you respond to requests from your customers about their data;
  • tell you without undue delay about any breach affecting it;
  • let you export it at any time, and delete it when you close your account, apart from records the law requires to be kept.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
